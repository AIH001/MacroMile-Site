GOOD TO KNOWQUICK ANSWERS

A few common questions.

Do I need an account?

No. MacroMile has no account or sign-up. Your log and profile are stored on your iPhone.

Can I use the app without AI features?

Yes. AI features are optional, and you can turn them off in the app. You can still track food and use the core weekly tools.

Where can I manage a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions.

Is MacroMile available on Android?

MacroMile is made for iPhone. There is no Android version at this time.
