01 / LOGGING

Log it how it happens.

Snap a meal, scan a barcode or nutrition label, search for a food, or enter it yourself. Photo estimates are shown as estimates, with room to review before you log.

02 / NUTRIENTS

See beyond macros.

The Nutrient Map brings vitamins, minerals, and more into view, while diet chat helps answer questions using your recent log. Missing nutrient data stays visible.

03 / ROUTINE

Keep the details together.

A LITTLE HELP, WHEN YOU WANT IT

A plan that can move with you.

Start with meals you already eat. Explore ideas that fit your goals. Ask for a change in plain language, preview it, and decide what happens next.

Meal planning, Photo Snap, the Nutrient Map, and diet chat are part of MacroMile Premium. Daily tracking and The Fix are available in the free experience.

BUILT WITH A LIGHTER TOUCH

The numbers should help you decide what’s next.

Targets are starting estimates. Photo logging shows its uncertainty. Nutrient gaps stay visible. And your plan changes only when you say so.

Your log lives on your iPhone. AI features ask before sending information and can be turned off in the app.
